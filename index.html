Notes on the data

Market numbers are Flipkart GMV and units by listing (FSN), buyer zone and seller zone for Jan–Oct 2026. The listing price field (fsp) in that file equals MRP for many listings and is far above what buyers paid, so this analysis uses GMV ÷ units as the real price. The market file has no size, material, rating or review fields, so size-level comparisons use our own data only. Company groups (Safari Industries, VIP Industries, Samsonite group, new-age D2C, fashion and lifestyle) were assigned by brand name. "GMV missed" assumes HRX would have kept its Jan–Jun share; it is an estimate, not a forecast. HRX’s Jan–Aug Flipkart sales are counted as our business; they come from the Flipkart file, because only Flipkart has data from January, while our master sheet starts in April; they are actual sales, but the Flipkart file has only GMV and units, so their net revenue (89.2% of GMV) and logistics (₹230 a unit) are calculated from our own Flipkart data, because they are not yet in the master sheet. Product cost (COGS) is not used anywhere, so our figures show revenue, not profit, and they exclude advertising spend, which is not in the master data.

Data: Flipkart suitcase market file (Jan–Oct 2026) and our master sheet (Amazon, Myntra, D2C from Apr 2026; Flipkart from Jan 2026). Confidential — for internal use.
